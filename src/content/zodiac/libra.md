Libra readings have a particular texture: lots of weighing, lots of "on the one hand", and a quiet hope that a card will just decide for you. Tarot will not do that, but it is very good at showing you which side of the scale you keep sneaking weight onto. Used well, it turns a loop of pros and cons into one clear picture.

## Your cards: Justice and the Queen of Swords

Libra's Major Arcana card is [Justice](/cards/justice). A crowned figure sits between two pillars holding a sword upright in one hand and a set of scales in the other. The sword cuts, the scales measure, and neither works without the other. It fits Libra because the sign is not really about being nice, it is about fairness, and fairness sometimes means an unpopular verdict. When Justice turns up, ask what the honest weighing looks like once you stop worrying about how it will land.

The court card for a cardinal air sign is the [Queen of Swords](/cards/queen-of-swords). She sits in profile on a high throne, one hand raised, a sword held straight up, clouds and a bare sky behind her. She is clear-eyed, articulate and not easily flattered. Libra often gets described as the agreeable one, but this queen is the agreeable one who has also thought it through and will say the difficult sentence calmly. If you have been softening something for weeks, she is the nudge to say it plainly and kindly.

## Love

Libra readings in love tend to circle around balance. Who texts first, who apologises more, who is doing the emotional admin. Cards that often show up are the [Two of Swords](/cards/two-of-swords), a blindfolded figure with crossed blades, and the [Two of Cups](/cards/two-of-cups), where two people exchange a cup under a winged lion. One is a stalemate, the other is real reciprocity, and the gap between them is usually the whole question.

The blind spot is peace-keeping. Libra will often absorb a small annoyance to avoid a scene, then be surprised when it has grown into a large one. The question to bring is not "does he like me?" but "what am I tolerating to keep things smooth?" If the pull is really about whether to stay, the [should I break up](/questions/should-i-break-up) page walks through that decision with cards. For the wider method, see the [love tarot reading guide](/guides/love-tarot-reading).

## Career and money

At work, Libra is the colleague everyone brings problems to, which can quietly become unpaid mediation. Look out for the [Eight of Swords](/cards/eight-of-swords), a bound and blindfolded figure in a ring of blades who, if you look closely, is not actually tied that tightly. It often means the constraint is more about indecision than circumstance.

On money, the [Seven of Pentacles](/cards/seven-of-pentacles) shows a figure leaning on a hoe, studying a bush of coins. It asks whether the effort is paying off or whether you are waiting for permission to reassess. Libra tends to avoid the awkward conversation about pay or workload. Justice and the Queen of Swords together say: put the facts on the table and ask for what is fair.

## Pulling a card for the month ahead

If you are pulling a card for the month ahead, do not ask what will happen. Ask where you are likely to over-accommodate. Draw one card and notice whether it shows someone acting or someone hesitating. A hesitating card, like the Two of Swords, suggests one decision you have been postponing. An acting card suggests you already know the answer.

A good monthly question: "Which choice am I avoiding, and what is it costing me?"

## Three spreads to try

- **The scales (three cards).** Left pan, right pan, and the fulcrum. Card one is the case for option A, card two the case for option B, card three what you are not accounting for. Use the [three card spread](/guides/three-card-spread) layout and decide the labels before you draw.
- **Fair exchange (love).** You, them, what is out of balance. Good for the moment a relationship feels lopsided but you cannot say why.
- **Decision in five.** A compact version of the [Celtic Cross](/guides/celtic-cross): situation, what you want, what you fear, advice, likely direction. It works when the decision is large and you have been circling it for weeks.

## Frequently asked questions

### What tarot card is Libra?

Libra's traditional Major Arcana card is [Justice](/cards/justice), which pictures a seated figure with a sword and scales. It reflects Libra's concern with fairness, weighing both sides and standing by an honest decision.

### What is the Libra court card?

The common modern pairing for Libra is the [Queen of Swords](/cards/queen-of-swords), since Libra is a cardinal air sign and cardinal signs map to Queens. She represents clear thinking, direct speech and independence.

### What spread is best for a Libra who cannot decide?

Try a three card spread with positions for each option and a third for what you are overlooking. It forces the weighing into the open instead of letting it loop in your head.
