Scorpio readings rarely stay on the surface, and honestly, that is why Scorpios tend to like tarot. You do not want the polite version, you want to know what is actually going on under the tidy story. The trick is to let the cards be blunt without treating blunt as final.

## Your cards: Death and the King of Cups

Scorpio's Major Arcana card is [Death](/cards/death), and yes, we should deal with that straight away. It does not predict a literal death. In the Rider-Waite picture a skeleton in black armour rides a white horse under a black banner carrying a white rose. A king lies fallen, a bishop pleads, a child offers flowers, and beyond them the sun rises between two towers. Everyone in the frame is facing the same fact: something is ending, and it is not up for negotiation. In readings it means a chapter closing, a role dropped, a relationship or habit that has already finished and is waiting for you to admit it. For Scorpio, a sign built on transformation, it is one of the most honest cards in the deck.

The court card for a fixed water sign is the [King of Cups](/cards/king-of-cups). He sits on a throne that floats on a choppy sea, a fish on a chain at his neck, a cup in one hand and a sceptre in the other. The water moves around him and he stays composed. That is the Scorpio ideal: deep feeling, held steady. It is also a warning, because a king who never lets the waves show can seem unreadable to the people closest to him.

## Love

Scorpio love readings go for the intense stuff: trust, loyalty, power, what was not said. Expect cards like the [Five of Cups](/cards/five-of-cups), a cloaked figure mourning three spilled cups while two stand upright behind, or the [Seven of Swords](/cards/seven-of-swords), someone tiptoeing away with a handful of blades. These show up when suspicion or secrecy is part of the picture, and the useful question is whether the secret is theirs or yours.

The blind spot is testing. A Scorpio will often set a quiet exam, watch whether the other person passes, and never say that an exam was happening. Try asking, "What do I need to say out loud instead of checking for?" If the real issue is whether someone is worth the intensity, the [is he the one](/questions/is-he-the-one) page is a good companion. The [love tarot reading guide](/guides/love-tarot-reading) covers how to frame these pulls without spiralling.

## Career and money

At work, Scorpio is the person who sees the real politics in the room and keeps it to themselves. The [Eight of Pentacles](/cards/eight-of-pentacles) fits well: a craftsman at a bench, stamping out coins one at a time, absorbed and unhurried. It rewards depth and patience, and it is a reminder that mastery is not the same as being noticed.

Money readings often surface control. The [Nine of Pentacles](/cards/nine-of-pentacles) shows a woman alone in a vineyard with a falcon on her glove, self-sufficient and at ease. For Scorpio it is less about wealth and more about the safety of needing nobody. Ask whether your independence is a choice or a guard.

## Pulling a card for the month ahead

If you are pulling a card for the month ahead, ask what needs to end, quietly, before something else can start. Scorpio months tend to hinge on one conversation or one release that has been put off. Watch for a card that shows someone leaving, shedding or turning away, and ask whether you are the one holding on.

A good monthly question: "What am I protecting that no longer needs protecting?" If the answer comes fast and uncomfortable, that is usually the right one.

## Three spreads to try

- **The shed skin (three cards).** What is finished, what is still clinging, what is ready to begin. A cleaner use of the [three card spread](/guides/three-card-spread) than past, present, future.
- **The unsaid (love).** What I feel, what I am not saying, what happens if I do. It suits the Scorpio habit of rehearsing a conversation for weeks without having it. See the [love tarot reading guide](/guides/love-tarot-reading) for variations.
- **Deep dive (Celtic Cross).** When something heavy needs the full picture, the [Celtic Cross](/guides/celtic-cross) has room for hidden influences and outcomes. Go slowly, and stop if it stops feeling useful.

## Frequently asked questions

### What tarot card is Scorpio?

Scorpio's traditional Major Arcana card is [Death](/cards/death). Despite the name it stands for endings and transformation, not literal death, which suits the sign's all-or-nothing relationship with change.

### What is the Scorpio court card?

The usual modern pairing is the [King of Cups](/cards/king-of-cups), because Scorpio is a fixed water sign and fixed signs map to Kings. He stands for emotional depth with self-control and the ability to stay steady while feeling a great deal.

### Is it bad if I pull Death as a Scorpio?

No. It is one of the cards people fear most and mean least. It usually points to something that has already run its course, and the reading is asking you to stop propping it up. Look at the sunrise in the background of the card, because it shows what comes next.
