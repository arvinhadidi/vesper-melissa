Taurus does not want a reading that dazzles. You want one that holds up when you look at it again on Thursday. Tarot for Taurus tends to be slow, sensory and practical, full of pentacles and the occasional card that politely asks whether you are staying put out of loyalty or out of habit.

## Your cards: The Hierophant and the King of Pentacles

Taurus is traditionally paired with [The Hierophant](/cards/the-hierophant). A robed teacher sits between two pillars, one hand raised in blessing, two kneeling figures at his feet and crossed keys in front of him. He is tradition, institutions and the way things have always been done. For Taurus it points at your relationship with the known and the trusted: the routine, the family way, the proper process.

Taurus is a fixed earth sign, so the common modern court card is the [King of Pentacles](/cards/king-of-pentacles). He sits on a throne carved with bulls, surrounded by grapevines, a castle behind him and a coin in his hand. He is the person who built something solid and enjoys it. When he turns up, the reading is often about security: whether you have it, whether you want more of it, and what you are paying for it.

## Love

Taurus in love is loyal, physical and slow to trust. A reading often shows someone who knows exactly what they like (the dinner, the weekend, the person who remembers how you take your tea) and is quietly unsure how to say what is missing. You can hold on to something well past its expiry because leaving feels like waste.

The cards that tend to appear are the [Four of Cups](/cards/four-of-cups), a figure under a tree ignoring a cup offered from a cloud, and the [Ten of Pentacles](/cards/ten-of-pentacles), a family scene with dogs, an archway and a patriarch in a long coat. The difference between them is usually whether you are still curious about the person.

Questions Taurus asks: "Is it normal to feel this settled?" and "Why can't I just let go?" Useful versions: "What would I want if I weren't afraid of losing what I have?" and "What am I treating as stability that is really avoidance?"

## Career and money

Taurus at work is the person who is reliably good and does not shout about it. Readings often show steady progress, then a stubborn plateau, because asking for the raise or the title feels vulgar. The [Eight of Pentacles](/cards/eight-of-pentacles), a craftsman carving coins one at a time, is the sign's natural card: skill built by repetition. The risk is that you keep carving while someone else gets promoted for turning up in the meeting.

On money, you are usually careful and occasionally lavish in specific, defensible ways (the good coat, the proper mattress). The [Four of Pentacles](/cards/four-of-pentacles) shows a figure clutching a coin to his chest with another under each foot. It asks whether you are protecting something or just refusing to let it move. If a move is on the table, the [should I move question](/questions/should-i-move) is a good place to see how that reads in cards.

## If you are pulling a card for the month ahead

Taurus months tend to be about pace. Pull one card for what deserves your patience and one for what has been waiting too long. If you see a lot of pentacles, the month is practical: money, home, body.

A monthly question that suits Taurus: "What would I change if I trusted that I would be fine?"

## Three spreads to try

- **Keep, release, grow** ([three card spread](/guides/three-card-spread)): one thing worth holding on to, one you are holding out of habit, one to invest in.
- **The comfort check** ([love tarot reading](/guides/love-tarot-reading)): what feels safe, what feels stale, what I would ask for. Three cards, read aloud, no skipping the middle one.
- **The yes or no with a cost** ([yes or no tarot](/guides/yes-or-no-tarot)): pull a card for yes, a card for no, and a card for what each would cost. Taurus decisions rarely hinge on the answer, they hinge on the price.

## Frequently asked questions

### What tarot card is Taurus?

Taurus is traditionally linked to The Hierophant, the robed teacher between two pillars. The pairing comes from the Golden Dawn system. It suits a sign that values tradition, routine and things that have been proven to work.

### What is the Taurus court card?

The usual modern choice is the King of Pentacles, since Taurus is a fixed earth sign and fixed signs map to Kings. He is pictured among bulls and grapevines, comfortable and successful. Treat it as a useful convention, not an official ruling.

### Can tarot tell a Taurus if they should stay or go?

Not in a fated way, but a spread can show what you are staying for and what it costs. Try keep, release and grow as three positions. The honest answer usually sits in the card for release.
