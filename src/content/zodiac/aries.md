Aries is the sign that pulls a card and wants to act on it before the deck is back in its box. Tarot for Aries works best when it slows you down by about ten minutes: not to talk you out of the instinct, but to check whether the instinct is a decision or just momentum. Expect readings full of wands, swords and a lot of forward motion.

## Your cards: The Emperor and the Queen of Wands

Aries is traditionally tied to [The Emperor](/cards/the-emperor). He sits on a stone throne carved with ram's heads, in a red robe, holding an ankh in one hand and an orb in the other, with bare mountains behind him. Nothing soft is pictured. It fits Aries because you tend to lead whether or not anyone appointed you. The lesson in the card is that real authority is steady, not loud.

For the court card, Aries is a cardinal fire sign, so the common modern pairing is the [Queen of Wands](/cards/queen-of-wands). She sits on a throne decorated with sunflowers and lions, a black cat at her feet, holding a single sunflower. She is warm, confident and slightly dangerous to cross. Where the Emperor commands, she attracts. If you draw her in a reading, you are probably being asked to use your charisma on purpose rather than by accident.

## Love

An Aries love reading usually surfaces speed. You decide quickly, you text first, and you can be bored by week three if nothing has been tested. The pattern the cards tend to show is a great opening followed by a stall, because the part that needs patience (the slow Tuesday, the awkward conversation) is not your favourite.

The blind spot is mistaking chase for compatibility. The [Knight of Wands](/cards/knight-of-wands) is the classic Aries-in-love card: a rider charging across a desert on a rearing horse, all heat and no map. The [Five of Wands](/cards/five-of-wands) shows five people waving staves with no clear enemy, which is often a couple arguing about the dishwasher when the real argument is about being heard.

Questions Aries tends to ask: "Why do I lose interest?" and "Am I too much?" Better ones: "What do I do when it stops being exciting?" and "What am I not saying out loud?" If you are weighing up whether to end something, the [should I break up question](/questions/should-i-break-up) walks through how a reading handles it.

## Career and money

Aries at work is a starter. You are brilliant at the first push and visibly restless in the maintenance phase. Readings often show the [Ace of Wands](/cards/ace-of-wands), a hand emerging from a cloud holding a budding staff, which is the card of a new idea with real energy behind it. The question it raises is who will still be doing this in six months.

Money readings for Aries lean towards impulse: the course bought at midnight, the flight booked in a mood. The [Eight of Wands](/cards/eight-of-wands), eight staves flying through clear sky, says things are about to move quickly, which is good news for a pitch and bad news for a half-finished spreadsheet.

## If you are pulling a card for the month ahead

Think of the month as a project you are leading, not a thing that happens to you. Pull one card for where your energy will be strongest and one for where it will leak. A run of wands all pointing the same way usually means you already know what you want.

A good monthly question for Aries: "What am I willing to finish before I start something new?"

## Frequently asked questions

### What tarot card is Aries?

Aries is traditionally linked to The Emperor, a seated ruler on a throne marked with rams' heads. The match comes from the Golden Dawn system, which paired each Major Arcana card with an astrological sign. It suits Aries because the card is about taking charge and building something with your own will.

### What is the Aries court card?

Many modern readers use the Queen of Wands, because Aries is a cardinal fire sign and cardinal signs map to Queens. She is confident, magnetic and a little fierce. It is a convention rather than a rule, so if another court card feels truer to you, trust that.

### Which spread is best for an Aries in love?

A simple three card spread with the positions impulse, cost and smarter move works well, because Aries readings tend to be about timing rather than feelings. If the question is about one person, try the you, them and relationship layout from the love tarot reading guide and read the middle card slowly.
