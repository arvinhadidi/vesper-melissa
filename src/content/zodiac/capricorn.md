Capricorn readings tend to be the most practical in the deck: what is the plan, what does it cost, what is being quietly carried? Capricorn is also the sign most likely to pull a card, say "that is probably nothing", and then think about it for three days. Good. That is how tarot works.

## Your cards: The Devil and the Queen of Pentacles

Capricorn's Major Arcana card is [The Devil](/cards/the-devil), and it looks worse than it is. A horned figure squats on a pedestal, with a naked man and woman chained loosely at the neck below him. Look at the chains: they are loose enough to lift off. The card is not about evil, it is about attachment, habit and the things we stay tied to out of routine, security or fear. For a hard-working, responsible sign, it asks a pointed question: which of your obligations are real, and which are just familiar? It is also an honest card about ambition, because wanting more is not a sin, but wanting it compulsively can be a trap.

The court card for a cardinal earth sign is the [Queen of Pentacles](/cards/queen-of-pentacles). She sits in a garden hung with roses, a pentacle on her lap, a rabbit at her feet. She is warm, capable and resourceful, and she makes a home out of whatever she is given. She fits Capricorn because the stereotype is cold ambition, but the truer picture is someone who builds things so that other people can rest in them.

## Love

Capricorn love readings often turn on the same quiet theme: the difficulty of being looked after. Cards that come up often include the [Four of Pentacles](/cards/four-of-pentacles), a figure clutching a coin with more under his feet and on his head, and the [Knight of Pentacles](/cards/knight-of-pentacles), a rider on a heavy horse in a ploughed field who is reliable and slow. One is closing off, the other is committing steadily, and a reading often shows you which one you are being.

The blind spot is treating a relationship like a project plan. You can be so reliable that the other person never sees what you need. A useful question is "what would I ask for if I believed it would be given?" If you are weighing a longer-term commitment, the [should I move](/questions/should-i-move) page covers the practical side of big life decisions. The [love tarot reading guide](/guides/love-tarot-reading) is a good place to start.

## Career and money

This is Capricorn's home turf. The [Ten of Pentacles](/cards/ten-of-pentacles) pictures a family under an archway, an old man with dogs and children, coins arranged like a tree of life. It is long-term security. The [Eight of Pentacles](/cards/eight-of-pentacles) is the daily grind that gets you there.

The challenge Capricorn readings tend to surface is not effort but rest. The Devil appears when work stops being a choice and becomes an identity. Ask whether you would still do this if nobody was grading it. When money is the topic, ask what safety actually means to you in numbers, not in feelings.

## Pulling a card for the month ahead

If you are pulling a card for the month ahead, ask what you are carrying that is not yours. Capricorn months go well when the to-do list has a clear edge and badly when everyone else's problems become your own. If the card shows work, ask where rest fits. If it shows chains or burdens, ask what could be put down.

A good monthly question: "What is one thing I can stop doing without anything collapsing?"

## Three spreads to try

- **The ledger (three cards).** What I am giving, what I am getting, what is out of balance. A practical use of the [three card spread](/guides/three-card-spread) for work or relationships.
- **The chain check.** For The Devil's question: what I feel bound to, what is actually holding it in place, what I could loosen. Use the [Celtic Cross](/guides/celtic-cross) if you want the extra depth.
- **Weekly pull.** One card each Sunday for the week ahead, following the [daily tarot reading guide](/guides/daily-tarot-reading) but at a pace that suits a busy diary.

## Frequently asked questions

### What tarot card is Capricorn?

Capricorn's traditional Major Arcana card is [The Devil](/cards/the-devil). It stands for attachment, habit and being tied to things you could in fact leave, which suits a sign known for duty and ambition.

### Is it bad to pull The Devil as a Capricorn?

No. It is not a prediction of harm. It is a prompt to look at what you are holding on to out of habit or fear, and the chains in the picture are loose on purpose. Most people find it one of the more useful cards once they stop flinching at it.

### What is the Capricorn court card?

The common modern pairing is the [Queen of Pentacles](/cards/queen-of-pentacles), since Capricorn is a cardinal earth sign and cardinal signs map to Queens. She is practical, nurturing and resourceful, building comfort for herself and others.
