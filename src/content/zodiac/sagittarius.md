Sagittarius is the sign that wants the big picture and the next horizon, so a Sagittarius reading is often less "what is wrong?" and more "where am I heading, and what am I skipping on the way?" Tarot is useful here as a brake pedal, a gentle one, not a lecture.

## Your cards: Temperance and the Knight of Wands

Sagittarius is traditionally matched to [Temperance](/cards/temperance). An angel with one foot on land and one in water pours liquid between two cups, an endless stream that never spills. A path winds off towards a glowing crown on distant mountains. It sounds like the opposite of a restless fire sign, but that is the point: Temperance is about blending extremes until they work, and Sagittarius at its best is the friend who can hold optimism and honesty in the same sentence.

The court card for a mutable fire sign is the [Knight of Wands](/cards/knight-of-wands). He rears up on a horse in a desert, in a tunic covered in salamanders, a flowering wand in his fist. He is all momentum and charm, and sometimes he arrives before the plan does. He fits Sagittarius because the energy is real and infectious, but the card is honest about the cost: he is also the one who books the flight before checking the dates.

## Love

Sagittarius love readings tend to circle freedom and honesty. You want a partner who is also a friend and a travel companion, and you bristle when closeness starts to feel like a cage. Look out for the [Ace of Wands](/cards/ace-of-wands), a hand offering a sprouting wand from a cloud, which often means a spark or fresh start. Then check what follows it. The [Three of Wands](/cards/three-of-wands) shows a figure looking out to sea at ships, which can mean long distance or simply a mind half somewhere else.

The blind spot is the exit sign. Sagittarius can be so quick to reframe a problem as "it is fine, I will see how I feel" that it never gets said. Try asking, "What conversation am I joking my way around?" The [will my ex come back](/questions/will-my-ex-come-back) page is a good read if the question is looking backwards. For general framing, the [love tarot reading guide](/guides/love-tarot-reading) helps keep the questions clean.

## Career and money

At work, Sagittarius gets bored when things stop growing. The [Six of Wands](/cards/six-of-wands) pictures a rider in a laurel wreath on a white horse through a cheering crowd, a good card for visible wins. The more useful question is what happens after the applause, when the next project is detail work. Sagittarius sometimes leaves just before the part that would turn a promising start into a track record.

Money readings often show generosity outrunning budget. The [Ten of Wands](/cards/ten-of-wands), a figure bent under a bundle of ten sticks, appears when you have said yes to too much. A practical use of tarot here is to ask what to hand back, not what to take on.

## Pulling a card for the month ahead

If you are pulling a card for the month ahead, ask where your attention keeps drifting and what it is drifting away from. Sagittarius months go well when there is one thing to aim at and badly when there are six half-started ones. If the card shows movement, ask what it is moving towards. If it shows stillness, ask what that stillness is trying to say.

A good monthly question: "What would I finish if I stopped starting new things?" Keep it to one answer.

## Three spreads to try

- **Aim and arrow (three cards).** Target, what is pulling me off course, the next adjustment. A tidy take on the [three card spread](/guides/three-card-spread) for a goal you keep announcing but not scheduling.
- **Yes, no or not yet.** Sagittarius is quick to say yes, so a single pull with the [yes or no tarot guide](/guides/yes-or-no-tarot) can be a useful pause before you commit to the trip, the move or the plan.
- **The daily compass.** One card each morning for a week, using the [daily tarot reading guide](/guides/daily-tarot-reading). Note what you did, not what you expected. It trains the habit of looking back, which is the thing fire signs skip.

## Frequently asked questions

### What tarot card is Sagittarius?

Sagittarius is traditionally linked to [Temperance](/cards/temperance), the angel pouring water between two cups. It stands for balance, moderation and finding the middle road towards a far-off goal.

### What is the Sagittarius court card?

The common modern pairing is the [Knight of Wands](/cards/knight-of-wands), because Sagittarius is a mutable fire sign and mutable signs map to Knights. He represents energy, adventure and enthusiasm, with a side of impatience.

### Which spread helps with a Sagittarius who feels restless?

A three card spread with positions for where you are aiming, what is pulling you off course and the next adjustment works well. It ties the restlessness to a direction instead of treating it as a problem. Keep the question specific, such as "what do I do about this job?", rather than "what should I do with my life?".
