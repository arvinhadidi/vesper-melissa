Leo is the sign that wants to be seen and secretly wants to be seen accurately. Tarot for Leo is less about predicting and more about checking whether the version of you in the room matches the version you feel. Expect wands, a few big Major Arcana cards, and at least one reading where the answer is simply: be kinder to yourself, and louder with the people who matter.

## Your cards: Strength and the King of Wands

Leo is traditionally paired with [Strength](/cards/strength). A woman in white, an infinity sign above her head, gently closes the mouth of a lion. She is not wrestling it. The card is about courage that looks like patience, and it suits Leo because the sign's real power is warmth, not volume. The lion in the picture is the pride, the temper and the appetite. The woman is what you do with them.

Leo is a fixed fire sign, so the common modern court card is the [King of Wands](/cards/king-of-wands). He sits on a throne carved with lions and salamanders, a living staff in hand and his cloak flowing. He is the person who sees a vision and brings others into it. He appears when your reading is about leadership, visibility or whether you are leading for the right reasons.

## Love

A Leo love reading tends to show generosity: the planned surprise, the loud compliment, the friend you defend in front of everyone. What you want back is loyalty and attention that is visible. The blind spot is pride. You may wait for them to say sorry first, or interpret a quiet week as a rejection.

The [Queen of Wands](/cards/queen-of-wands) and the [Sun](/cards/the-sun) often show up here. The Sun is a child on a white horse under a huge sun with sunflowers behind, pure uncomplicated joy, and it is what Leo tends to look for in love. The [Five of Cups](/cards/five-of-cups) is the opposite: a cloaked figure mourning three spilled cups while two stand upright behind. It is the card of sulking in a grief that has already been survived.

Questions Leo asks: "Do they appreciate me?" and "Why am I the one who always plans?" Better ones: "Have I asked for what I want in words?" and "What would I do if applause were not part of it?"

## Career and money

Leo at work is a natural front-of-room person. You are brilliant when you can own something, and miserable when you are in the background doing the unseen version of someone else's idea. Readings often show the [Three of Wands](/cards/three-of-wands), a figure on a cliff watching ships sail out, a plan in motion and a wider view. The question is whether you are directing the ships or only waving them off.

On money, Leo can be wonderful at generosity and weak at boundaries: the round of drinks, the favour, the "I'll cover it." Strength asks whether you are giving from fullness or from a need to be seen as generous. If you are waiting on an answer about a role, the [will I get the offer question](/questions/will-i-get-the-offer) shows how a reading handles nerves without making promises.

## If you are pulling a card for the month ahead

A Leo month often turns on recognition. Pull one card for where your energy will be seen and one for where you need to be steady and unseen. Notice any lion imagery, any sun, any figure on a throne: they usually mean the month is about taking up your place with grace. A run of cups suggests it is about asking for warmth instead of waiting for it.

A good monthly question for Leo: "Where am I performing when I could just be present?"

## Three spreads to try

- **The lion and the tamer** ([three card spread](/guides/three-card-spread)): the instinct, the response, the outcome. Use it before a confrontation.
- **Seen and unseen** ([love tarot reading](/guides/love-tarot-reading)): what I give, what I need, what I have not asked for. Leo readings are often hiding in the third card.
- **A yes or no with pride** ([yes or no tarot](/guides/yes-or-no-tarot)): pull one card for yes, one for no, and one for what your ego wants. If the ego card disagrees with both, you have your answer.

## Frequently asked questions

### What tarot card is Leo?

Leo is traditionally linked to Strength, the card of a woman calmly closing a lion's mouth. The pairing comes from the Golden Dawn system. It fits because Leo's power is warmth and courage, not force.

### What is the Leo court card?

The usual modern choice is the King of Wands, since Leo is a fixed fire sign and fixed signs map to Kings. He sits on a throne carved with lions and salamanders. It is a convention, and the Queen of Wands works well if the question is about charisma.

### What does a Leo reading usually reveal?

Often the gap between how you show up and how you feel. Leo tends to give generously and wait to be noticed. A short spread with positions for what you give, what you need and what you have not asked for tends to bring that out clearly.
