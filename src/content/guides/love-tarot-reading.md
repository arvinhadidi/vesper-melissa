A love tarot reading works best when you stop asking the deck to read his mind and start asking it to read the situation. Cards are brilliant at showing what you are avoiding, what is unsaid and what you keep hoping will change. They are useless at telling you what someone is thinking while he watches football.

## Questions that work and questions that do not

The question is most of the reading. Compare:

| Does not work | Works better |
| --- | --- |
| Does he love me? | What do I need to understand about where we are? |
| Will my ex come back? | What is keeping me stuck on this? |
| Is he cheating? | What is making me feel unsafe in this relationship? |
| When will I meet someone? | What is getting in the way of me meeting people right now? |

The right-hand versions put you at the centre. They also give you something you can do on Thursday, rather than a prediction to wait around for. Yes/no versions can work for decisions, and our [yes or no tarot guide](/guides/yes-or-no-tarot) shows how to phrase them.

## A five-card love spread

Lay five cards in a row, or in a cross shape if you like symmetry.

1. **You.** How you are showing up in this right now.
2. **Them.** What they are bringing, as far as you can observe. This describes their behaviour, not their secret thoughts.
3. **The connection.** What is actually happening between you.
4. **The block.** What is getting in the way.
5. **The next step.** One thing you can do.

Read the cards as a story, left to right. If card one is the [Queen of Cups](/cards/queen-of-cups) and card two is the [Knight of Swords](/cards/knight-of-swords), you might be someone who feels everything, with someone who communicates in short bursts and then disappears. That is a mismatch you can name, not a flaw in either of you.

If you would like a longer layout, try the [Celtic Cross](/guides/celtic-cross) for a heavy question.

## Key love cards to know

- [The Lovers](/cards/the-lovers): choice and choosing on the basis of shared values. It is less about romance than about picking someone on purpose.
- [Two of Cups](/cards/two-of-cups): mutual attraction, two people meeting in the middle.
- [Ten of Cups](/cards/ten-of-cups): settled, family-level contentment.
- [Three of Swords](/cards/three-of-swords): heartbreak, or a truth that hurts.
- [The Devil](/cards/the-devil): a pull you cannot explain, often the situationship with great chemistry and no plan.
- [The Moon](/cards/the-moon): something is unclear, or you are filling gaps with imagination.
- [Knight of Cups](/cards/knight-of-cups): the romantic, the charming offer. Check whether it arrives with follow-through.
- [Eight of Cups](/cards/eight-of-cups): walking away from something that no longer feels enough.

## Reading for an ex

Do not ask whether he will return. Ask what the relationship taught you, what you are still waiting for, and what closure would look like if it came from you. A [Five of Cups](/cards/five-of-cups) often shows up here: three spilled cups in front, two upright ones behind. It asks you to turn around and notice what remains.

If you keep pulling the same card about the same person, that is a sign the real question is not about him. If you still want to ask it, [will my ex come back?](/questions/will-my-ex-come-back) has a spread built for exactly that, and [is he the one?](/questions/is-he-the-one) covers the bigger question.

## Reading for a situationship

Situationships tend to produce cards like the Moon, the Devil and the Two of Swords. Ask: "What do I want from this, and am I getting it?" Then compare your answer to what you actually receive. Perhaps you get a text on Sunday and a plan on never. The cards will not fix that, but they can help you say it out loud.

## Reading when you are single

Single readings are about readiness and habits. Good questions include "What pattern do I keep repeating when I date?" and "What would make me feel more open this month?" You might draw the [Hermit](/cards/the-hermit) and realise you have been declining invitations since March.

## Reading when you are together

In a relationship, ask about communication. "What is the thing we are not talking about?" is a good one. If you pull the Moon, it may be a subject neither of you will raise at dinner. Pair the reading with an actual conversation, ideally one that does not happen in the car.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can tarot tell me if someone loves me?

Tarot cannot read another person's mind or promise how they feel. It can show what you are noticing, what is unspoken and where the relationship feels uneven. Ask what you need to understand, then judge by how they behave over time, not by one card.

### Is it okay to read tarot about an ex?

Yes, as long as the aim is understanding rather than monitoring. Questions about what you learned or what you need to release work well. If you check the cards every day for signs of a return, it is probably keeping you stuck.

### What is the best love tarot spread for beginners?

A simple three card spread works well: you, them and the connection. It is short enough to read clearly and gives you something to reflect on. Once you are comfortable, move to the five-card layout above for more detail.
